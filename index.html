Ruizhi Chang

I am a research assistant at City University of Hong Kong, where I work on real-to-sim scene reconstruction for robot manipulation. Before that, I was a research engineer at Xiaomi EV, where I developed a learning-based planner deployed on over 100,000 production vehicles and worked on vision-language models for autonomous driving. I received my M.Sc. from Jilin University, where I worked on LiDAR–visual–inertial odometry.

My research interests are 3D world models and scene understanding for robot learning and end-to-end autonomous driving. I am applying for PhD programs starting in Fall 2027.
